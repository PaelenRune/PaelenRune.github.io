RUNES DRAWN BY FATE

CHARACTERS & LORE

Meet the people and world introduced in Chapter One.

Characters

Main character

Paelen Arven

Helps in his family's workshop in Turkuaz Eşik. He is drawn to alchemy and rune architecture.

Paelen's mother

Haera Arven

Manages the family workshop's budget, orders, and market stall.

Paelen's father

Faelar Arven

Works on production and repairs in the workshop, with a keen instinct for mechanics.

Paelen's childhood friend

Ferion Deryan

Works as a deckhand aboard the merchant ship Mavi Kırlangıç.

A glimpse of the world

Turkuaz Eşik

A sunlit coastal city of the Larantya Kingdom, shaped by cliffs, markets, and sea trade.

The family workshop

The Arven family makes souvenirs and art pieces, selling them at the tourist market.

Rune architecture

The discipline of designing, developing, and adapting runes for different purposes.
